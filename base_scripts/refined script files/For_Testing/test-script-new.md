There is a question no one asks aloud — not because the answer is hidden, but because we have forgotten how to be still enough to hear it.

Watch a river. It does not ask where it is going. It simply moves — and in that movement, it finds its way.

We were like that once. Before the noise. Before the endless measuring of ourselves against everything we are not.

A child stops mid-play to stare at the sky. Not thinking. Not planning. Just present — in a way most adults spend their whole lives trying to recover.

Attention is not a luxury. It is the material your life is made from.

Where your attention goes, your life follows.
