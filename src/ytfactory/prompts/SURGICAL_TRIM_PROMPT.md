## Core Principle

Preserve the script's architecture.

The reader should experience the exact same story, in the exact same order, with the exact same emotional progression. The trimmed version should feel like the original script with unnecessary words removed—not like a rewritten or simplified version.

## Immutable Sections

The following sections are considered structurally critical and must remain intact unless they alone exceed the target length:

- Opening hook
- Rehooks
- Story setup
- Major story beats
- Emotional turning points
- Climax
- Philosophical payoff
- Callback
- Closing takeaway
- Final CTA (the three closing lines: "This is the Atma Theory." / "If these ideas resonate with you, join us on this journey." / "Clear mind. Meaningful life.")

Do not remove, merge, reorder, or substantially rewrite these sections.

## Allowed Edits

Make the smallest possible edits required to reach the target length.

Always prefer, in this order:

1. Remove redundant words.
2. Remove repeated ideas.
3. Remove unnecessary adjectives and adverbs.
4. Compress verbose sentences.
5. Merge adjacent sentences expressing the same idea.
6. Simplify wording while preserving meaning.
7. Remove minor supporting details that do not affect the narrative or philosophy.

## Never

- Never delete an entire paragraph unless it is genuinely redundant.
- Never remove an entire story beat.
- Never remove transitions.
- Never shorten the hook for convenience.
- Never shorten the climax.
- Never shorten the philosophical payoff.
- Never remove callbacks.
- Never remove emotional setup or emotional payoff.
- Never change the order of ideas.
- Never introduce new ideas.
- Never change the author's philosophy or voice.
- Never sacrifice emotional impact simply to save words.

## Compression Strategy

Prefer dozens of tiny edits over a few large deletions.

Every edit should have a clear editorial justification:
- removes redundancy,
- improves conciseness,
- or eliminates unnecessary repetition.

If a sentence is already strong and concise, leave it unchanged.

When in doubt, preserve the original wording.
