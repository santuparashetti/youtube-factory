==================================================

==================================================
VIEWER RETENTION
==================================================

The question planted in the hook is an open loop. Keep it open. Resolve it only near the very end, so its answer becomes part of the climax. A viewer stays to close a loop.

Never place more than two examples of the same shape back to back. If several stories carry the same lesson, break the pattern before the next begins — with a direct question to the viewer, a modern moment, or a beat of stillness. Repetition of structure tires the viewer even when the words are different.

The middle is where attention fades. Guard it. Roughly every ninety seconds, something must shift — the rhythm, the register, the perspective, or the stakes. Nothing in the middle should feel like more of the same.
