# ATMA THEORY - MASTER THUMBNAIL VISUAL STYLE GUIDE

## Core Visual Identity

Minimalist philosophical storybook illustration with a highly
recognizable recurring character.

The thumbnail should feel like:

> **One simple illustrated story + one powerful emotional idea + huge
> readable typography.**

Do not make thumbnails look like detailed movie posters. The character
and the idea are the stars.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 1. Canvas & Composition

-   **Aspect ratio:** 16:9
-   **Resolution:** 1672 × 941 px
-   Keep important elements inside safe margins.
-   No unnecessary borders or logos unless specifically requested.
-   Character and text should generally occupy opposite sides.
-   The layout may flip when the story action requires it.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 2. Anchor Character - LOCKED

The recurring boy is the visual mascot of Atma Theory.

### Character identity

-   Young child with a small, gentle build.
-   Short pale/white-blond hair.
-   Round black glasses.
-   Large expressive dark eyes.
-   Warm **medium/tan skin tone**.
-   Small, soft facial features.
-   Innocent but thoughtful appearance.
-   Never make him extremely muscular, fashionable, glamorous, or overly
    mature.

### Absolute consistency

-   Keep the same face.
-   Keep the same hairstyle.
-   Keep the same glasses.
-   Keep the same skin tone.
-   Keep the same proportions.
-   Keep the same recognizable character design.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 3. Clothing - LOCKED

The clothing should remain essentially identical across thumbnails:

-   Cream/off-white traditional tunic.
-   Cream/off-white loose trousers.
-   Light cream scarf/shawl.
-   Subtle muted reddish/peach stripes on the scarf.
-   Brown leather sandals.
-   No jewelry.
-   No hats.
-   No modern accessories.
-   No costume changes.

> **Do not change the character's clothing based on the story. The story
> changes around the character.**

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 4. Character Pose - VARIABLE

Do **not** use the same thinking pose in every thumbnail.

Instead:

> **Same character + different meaningful action.**

The action should visually represent the video's central idea.

Examples:

  Video idea        Character action
  ----------------- -----------------------------
  Trust             Looking upward
  Greed             Reaching toward a gold coin
  Contentment       Sleeping peacefully
  Desire            Reaching for something
  Comparison        Looking between two objects
  Fear              Holding himself tightly
  Letting go        Open hands
  Overthinking      Sitting with head lowered
  Freedom           Walking/running
  Ambition          Climbing
  Enough            Relaxing
  "Just One More"   Swimming toward a coin

The gesture is storytelling, not decoration.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 5. Background Style

Background should be:

-   Clean.
-   Light.
-   Soft.
-   Warm.
-   Minimal.
-   Slightly illustrated.
-   Low visual noise.

### Preferred background

-   Warm ivory.
-   Cream.
-   Pale beige.
-   Soft blue.
-   Restrained natural landscape tones.

### Good environments

-   Simple meadow.
-   Quiet river.
-   Plain sky.
-   Minimal room.
-   Simple field.
-   Wooden chair.
-   Subtle landscape.

### Avoid

-   Crowded environments.
-   Complicated architecture.
-   Dozens of objects.
-   Excessive treasure.
-   Busy scenery.
-   Too many animals.
-   Multiple competing characters.
-   Overly detailed backgrounds.

The viewer should understand the visual idea in **less than one
second**.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 6. Background Lighting

Use a soft cinematic illustration lighting system.

Preferred:

-   Warm sunlight.
-   Soft golden-hour glow.
-   Gentle shadows.
-   Subtle atmospheric depth.
-   Clean highlights.

Do not overdo the orange/gold treatment.

For sky-focused thumbnails, prefer:

> Clear blue sky → warm horizon → simple mountains → sunlight.

Avoid cloud-filled skies that make the sky itself visually cluttered.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 7. Visual Story Rule

Every thumbnail should contain **one visual metaphor**.

Not five.

### Examples

**JUST ONE MORE**

Character swimming → giant gold coin.

**WHY MORE NEVER FEELS ENOUGH**

Character sleeping comfortably → thought bubble with one gold-filled
chest.

**YOU MADE FOR THE SKY**

Character → eagle overhead.

This is the core rule:

> **ONE CHARACTER + ONE IDEA + ONE SYMBOL.**

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 8. Supporting Objects

Supporting objects should be large and immediately understandable.

### Good

-   One gold coin.
-   One treasure chest.
-   One wooden chair.
-   One eagle.
-   One mirror.
-   One door.
-   One mountain.
-   One cup.
-   One book.

### Bad

-   Treasure chest + coins + jewelry + palace + car + crown + multiple
    people.

The third reference demonstrated the right direction:

> **One treasure chest full of gold coins.**

Keep that simplicity.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 9. Text Panel

Use:

-   Solid black.
-   Rounded corners.
-   Large rectangular/stepped panel.
-   Very high contrast.
-   No gradient.
-   No complicated effects.

The panel should feel like a bold editorial poster placed into the
illustrated scene.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 10. Typography

### Font character

Use a:

> **Very bold condensed sans-serif uppercase font.**

Visual characteristics:

-   Tall.
-   Heavy.
-   Condensed.
-   Extremely readable.
-   Strong vertical presence.

Reference feel:

-   IMPACT
-   Anton
-   Bebas Neue
-   Similar heavy condensed display fonts.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 11. Text Length

This should be a hard rule.

### Maximum:

**3 words whenever possible.**

Ideally:

**2-3 words.**

Examples:

-   JUST ONE MORE
-   NEVER ENOUGH
-   STOP WANTING MORE
-   TRUST THE FLOW
-   START LIVING
-   LIVE FULLY
-   CHOOSE YOURSELF

The thumbnail should **not explain the video**.

The video title and thumbnail work together.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 12. Text Colors

Use only two text colors.

### Primary

**White**

Used for most words.

### Accent

**Bright yellow**

Target approximately:

**#FDD800**

This yellow should become a recognizable Atma Theory accent.

### Standard hierarchy

Usually:

> **WHITE / WHITE / YELLOW**

Example:

``` text
WHY MORE
NEVER FEELS
ENOUGH
```

Where `ENOUGH` is yellow.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 13. Yellow Rule

Do not use:

-   Orange-gold.
-   Mustard.
-   Dark yellow.

Use a:

> **Bright, saturated yellow approximately #FDD800.**

It needs to remain visible even when the thumbnail is displayed very
small.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 14. Text Hierarchy

One word should usually carry the emotional punch.

That word gets yellow.

Example:

``` text
WHY MORE
NEVER FEELS
ENOUGH
```

The yellow word should generally be:

-   The emotional focus.
-   One of the largest words.
-   The strongest visual accent.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 15. Text / Character Position

The composition does **not** have to permanently be:

> Text left + character right.

Instead:

> **Text and character should generally occupy opposite sides.**

### Option A

`TEXT | CHARACTER`

### Option B

`CHARACTER | TEXT`

Choose based on the action.

For a swimming scene:

> **CHARACTER → GOLD COIN → TEXT**

may work better than forcing the character to the right.

The text should never cover the important action.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 16. Character Scale

Character should be large enough for the viewer to immediately
understand the face and action.

Usually:

**35-50% of thumbnail height**, depending on the action.

Do not make him tiny.

The character should feel like the recurring protagonist.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 17. Face

The face is part of the brand.

Maintain:

-   Same basic face.
-   Same hairstyle.
-   Same glasses.
-   Same skin tone.
-   Same proportions.
-   Same gentle facial design.

But the emotion can change:

-   Peaceful.
-   Curious.
-   Surprised.
-   Determined.
-   Worried.
-   Joyful.
-   Sleepy.
-   Contemplative.

The face should remain recognizable.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 18. Illustration Style

### Character

**Hand-painted storybook / polished digital illustration**

with:

-   Clean outlines.
-   Soft painterly shading.
-   Warm skin rendering.
-   Expressive eyes.
-   Slightly exaggerated proportions.
-   Cinematic lighting.

### Environment

Also illustrated, but simpler and softer.

The character should have slightly stronger visual definition than the
environment.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 19. Realism Level

Do **not** move toward photorealism.

The channel identity should remain:

> **Premium illustrated philosophical storybook.**

Avoid:

-   Realistic photography.
-   Anime.
-   Flat vector.
-   Generic children's cartoon.
-   Overly glossy 3D CGI.

The intended balance is:

> **Cinematic + illustrated + philosophical + storybook.**

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 20. Emotional Contrast

The thumbnail should often contain a contradiction.

Examples:

### WHY MORE NEVER FEELS ENOUGH

Peaceful sleeping child + enormous wealth dream.

### JUST ONE MORE

Child struggling through water + single giant gold coin.

### YOU MADE FOR THE SKY

Small child on ground + eagle flying freely above.

This creates curiosity.

The viewer subconsciously asks:

> **"Why are these two things together?"**

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 21. Clutter Limit

Target:

> **1 anchor character + 1 main symbolic object + simple environment +
> text panel.**

One secondary element is acceptable when genuinely useful.

Avoid:

-   Treasure piles.
-   Many animals.
-   Multiple characters.
-   Elaborate buildings.
-   Excessive decorative objects.

The direction should always be toward **simplification**, not increasing
detail.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 22. Thumbnail Visual Formula

Use this formula for every future video:

> **RECURRING CHARACTER + STORY-SPECIFIC ACTION + ONE SYMBOLIC OBJECT +
> SIMPLE LIGHT BACKGROUND + BLACK TEXT PANEL + WHITE/YELLOW TYPOGRAPHY**

This is the master formula.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 23. Master Generation Prompt Structure

For every future thumbnail prompt, establish the character first:

> Create a 16:9 Atma Theory YouTube thumbnail using the established
> recurring boy character. Preserve his exact recognizable face, pale
> short hair, round glasses, warm medium/tan skin tone, cream
> traditional tunic and trousers, cream scarf with subtle muted stripes,
> and brown leather sandals. Do not change his clothing, skin tone,
> hairstyle, glasses, or core character design.

Then define the story action:

> Give the character a new story-specific action/gesture appropriate to
> this video's theme.

Then define the symbol:

> Use one simple visual metaphor related to the video.

Then define the environment:

> Keep the background light, warm, clean, minimal and uncluttered.

Then define the typography:

> Place a large black rounded text panel on the side opposite the
> character. Use maximum three words in bold condensed uppercase
> typography. Most words are white, with one emotionally important word
> in bright yellow approximately #FDD800.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 24. Negative Rules

Every future thumbnail should avoid:

-   Character redesign.
-   Clothing changes.
-   Skin-tone changes.
-   Hairstyle changes.
-   Glasses changes.
-   Multiple protagonists.
-   Busy backgrounds.
-   Photorealism.
-   Anime style.
-   Excessive gold.
-   Excessive clouds.
-   Excessive props.
-   Tiny typography.
-   More than 3 words.
-   Multiple competing symbols.
-   Complicated text effects.
-   Gradients inside text.
-   Unnecessary logos.
-   Religious symbols unless the story genuinely requires them.
-   Generic stock-art appearance.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 25. Reference Trio

These three thumbnails are the master reference set.

### Reference 1 - Freedom / aspiration

**YOU MADE FOR THE SKY**

Character + eagle + clean landscape.

### Reference 2 - Greed / endless desire

**JUST ONE MORE**

Character swimming + single giant coin.

### Reference 3 - Contentment / abundance

**WHY MORE NEVER FEELS ENOUGH**

Character sleeping + single treasure chest.

Together they define the system:

**SAME CHARACTER**\
↓\
**SAME BRAND CLOTHING**\
↓\
**SAME TYPOGRAPHY**\
↓\
**SAME BLACK + WHITE + YELLOW SYSTEM**\
↓\
**SAME CLEAN BACKGROUND PHILOSOPHY**\
↓\
**DIFFERENT ACTION**\
↓\
**DIFFERENT SYMBOL**\
↓\
**DIFFERENT EMOTION**

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Final Brand Principle

> **Don't make the thumbnails identical. Make them recognizable.**

The boy is the constant.

**The story is the variable.**

That gives Atma Theory a visual identity while preventing the channel
from looking repetitive.
